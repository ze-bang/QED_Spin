\begin{abstract}
This is the complete technical reference for the QED exact-diagonalization
engine after the Stage 9--11 consolidation. The pipeline is one sentence
--- \emph{given a Hamiltonian: find the symmetries, build the basis, do the
matvec, run the Lanczos} --- and after the consolidation every step has
exactly one implementation. This document derives the mathematics each step
rests on (monomial group actions, representative bases and their norms, the
$A' = A \rtimes \Z_2$ flip extension, little co-groups and isotypic
projection, time-reversal folds, Krylov convergence and the ghost-Ritz
mechanism, finite-temperature estimators, continued-fraction spectral
functions) and pairs each derivation with the code that implements it,
quoted from the tree. The final sections cover the cross-cutting guards
(covering sum rules, commutator probes, residual gates, content-hash
verification), the two July hardenings, and the measured validation
numbers.
\end{abstract}

\section{The problem and the design invariant}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{Setting}

Spin-$\tfrac12$ models on $N$-site clusters. The computational basis is the
$\sigma^z$ product basis, identified with bitstrings $s \in \{0,1\}^N \cong
\{0,\dots,2^N-1\}$; site $i$ is up iff bit $i$ of $s$ is set. The
Hamiltonian is a sum of one-, two- and three-body terms,
\[
  H \;=\; \sum_t c_t \, O_t, \qquad
  O_t \in \{S^z_i,\ S^\pm_i,\ S^z_iS^z_j,\ S^+_iS^-_j,\ S^\pm_iS^\pm_j,\ \dots\},
\]
stored as structure-of-arrays term lists (\file{ed/matvec/term\_storage.h}).
Every solver consumes $H$ only through its action $v \mapsto Hv$
(``matvec''); no full matrix is ever required, though \emph{reduced} sector
matrices are assembled when they fit a budget (\S\ref{sec:matvec}).

At the frontier ($N = 36$, half filling) the $U(1)$ block has dimension
$\binom{36}{18} \approx 9.1\times 10^9$: a single complex vector is
$\sim$145\,GB. Everything below exists to divide that number by the
symmetry group and to keep the working set at $O(\#\text{representatives})$.

\subsection{The invariant that generates every layer}
\label{sec:invariant}

\begin{prop}[project vs.\ fold]\label{prop:projfold}
Let $\Hs = \bigoplus_b \Hs_b$ be a decomposition preserved by $H$, and let
$U$ be a unitary with $[H,U]=0$.
(i) If $U\Hs_b = \Hs_b$, then $U|_{\Hs_b}$ generates a further orthogonal
decomposition of $\Hs_b$ into eigenspaces of $U$ (\emph{projection}: smaller
blocks).
(ii) If $U\Hs_b = \Hs_{b'}$ with $b' \ne b$, then
$H|_{\Hs_{b'}} = U H|_{\Hs_b} U^\dagger$ is unitarily equivalent to
$H|_{\Hs_b}$ (\emph{fold}: one solve, copied spectrum, multiplicity 2).
(iii) An antiunitary $K$ with $KHK^{-1}=H$ never projects: $K$ has no
eigenspaces in the linear sense; its entire exploitable content is the fold
$H|_{K\Hs_b} \cong \overline{H|_{\Hs_b}}$, which for Hermitian $H$ is
isospectral.
\end{prop}

The stack is the maximal assignment of these three roles:
\[
\text{one solve} \;=\;
\underbrace{\bigl(U(1)\ n_\uparrow \ \text{or}\ S^z\text{-parity}\bigr)}_{\text{subspace}}
\times
\underbrace{\bigl(A' = A \rtimes \Z_2^{\text{flip}}\bigr) \otimes P_{k_0}}_{\text{projector}}
\times
\underbrace{\bigl(\text{stars} \cdot \text{TR} \cdot \text{transport}\bigr)}_{\text{folds}}.
\]

\section{Group action and the representative basis}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{Monomial action on bitstrings}
\label{sec:action}

The exploited symmetries act on the computational basis by \emph{site
permutations composed with global bit-flips}. An element
$g = (\pi_g, f_g)$, with $\pi_g \in S_N$ and $f_g \in \{0, \texttt{all-ones}\}$,
acts as
\[
  U_g \ket{s} = \ket{g(s)}, \qquad
  g(s) \;=\; \pi_g(s) \oplus f_g ,
\]
a pure permutation of basis states (no phases: $\prod_i \sigma^x_i$ maps a
product state to a product state with coefficient $+1$). The group $G$ is
therefore realized by 0/1 monomial matrices, and everything downstream ---
orbits, stabilizers, norms --- is combinatorics on bitstrings.

\textbf{Implementation.} \file{ed/symmetry/compiled\_group.h} compiles each
element to a byte-lookup table: the permutation is applied one input byte at
a time ($\mathrm{bpw} = \lceil N/8 \rceil$ table loads), then one XOR
applies the flip:

\begin{lstlisting}
/// Action of element ``g`` on state ``s``. BPW table loads + one XOR.
[[nodiscard]] inline std::uint64_t
apply(std::uint64_t s, std::size_t g) const noexcept {
    const std::uint64_t* lut_g = lut_.data() + g * stride_;
    std::uint64_t out = 0;
    for (int b = 0; b < bpw_; ++b) {
        out |= lut_g[(static_cast<std::size_t>(b) << 8)
                     + ((s >> (8 * b)) & 0xFFu)];
    }
    return out ^ flips_[g];
}
\end{lstlisting}

The bit-identity contract (\texttt{apply(s,g) ==
applyPermutation(s, perm[g]) \^{} flips[g]}) is pinned against the scalar
reference by \file{test\_compiled\_group.cpp} ($\sim$17k assertions).

\subsection{Orbits, stabilizers, projected states}
\label{sec:repbasis}

Fix an abelian group $A$ (the maximal abelian subgroup of the automorphism
group, extended by the flip where admissible, \S\ref{sec:flip}) with
characters $\chi_k : A \to U(1)$. For each orbit
$\mathcal{O}_r = \{a(r) : a \in A\}$ choose the representative
$r = \min \mathcal{O}_r$ (integer order on bitstrings). The projected state
in irrep $k$ is
\begin{equation}
  \ket{r; k} \;=\; \frac{1}{\sqrt{|A|\, \nu_r(k)}}
    \sum_{a \in A} \overline{\chi_k(a)}\, U_a \ket{r},
  \qquad
  \nu_r(k) \;=\; \sum_{h \in \Stab(r)} \chi_k(h),
  \label{eq:projstate}
\end{equation}
where $\Stab(r) = \{h \in A : h(r) = r\}$. Since $A$ is abelian and
$\chi_k$ is a homomorphism, $\nu_r(k) = |\Stab(r)|$ if $\chi_k$ restricts
trivially to $\Stab(r)$ and $0$ otherwise --- the \emph{survival
condition}. The basis of the $(n_\uparrow, k)$ sector is
$\{\ket{r;k} : \nu_r(k) > 0\}$, of dimension
$D_k = \#\{\text{surviving reps}\}$, and Burnside's lemma gives the
covering sum rule used as a runtime tripwire (\S\ref{sec:guards}):
\begin{equation}
  \sum_k D_k \;=\; \binom{N}{n_\uparrow}.
  \label{eq:burnside}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Implementation.} A single fused scan
(\file{ed/symmetry/orbit\_table.h}) enumerates representatives \emph{and}
stabilizers per $(\text{subspace}, G)$ pair --- the \texttt{OrbitTable}.
The scan walks the subspace in ascending state order; for each state $s$ it
computes the $|G|$ images with the byte-LUT \texttt{apply}, early-exits the
moment any image is $< s$ (then $s$ is not a representative --- this is the
dominant, cheap branch), and otherwise records $s$ as a representative
together with the stabilizer bitmask $\{g : g(s) = s\}$ collected in the
same pass. A closed-form popcount prefilter skips states whose bit count
cannot match the subspace before any group work. Stabilizer masks are
deduplicated (\texttt{StabDedup}): lattices have few distinct stabilizer
subgroups, so per-rep storage is an index into a small table. The whole
artifact is cached in-process by content hash and on disk as
\texttt{<lattice>/basis\_cache/sym\_v2/<hash>.otab}
(\S\ref{sec:hashing}); a warm $N{=}28$ run loads $1.43$M representatives
from a $14$\,MB file instead of rescanning.

Per irrep, the $O(\#\text{reps})$ view is a \texttt{RepSectorData}: sorted
\texttt{reps[]}, $\texttt{inv\_norms}[i] = \nu_{r_i}(k)^{-1/2}$ evaluated
in closed form from the stored stabilizer
($\nu = \sum_{h \in \Stab} \chi_k(h)$, zero-norm reps dropped --- the
survival condition), the $|A'|$ characters, the flattened permutations, and
(when the flip is engaged) per-element XOR masks. Construction is
\emph{lazy-only} since Stage 11c-1: the builders in \file{sector\_set.h}
hand every sector a \texttt{RepSectorData} provider
(\texttt{SectorBasis::configureRepLazy}), and the legacy per-sector ``orbit
CSR'' exists only as an on-demand product for orbit-\emph{data} consumers
(\texttt{SectorBasis::build} behind \texttt{ensureHostCsr}: the
dense-assembly decline path and cross-sector observables).

\subsection{Matrix elements: the representative walk}
\label{sec:repwalk}

Let a Hamiltonian term act as $O_t \ket{r_i} = c_t \ket{s'}$ (off-diagonal
terms connect one basis state to one basis state). Projecting
$H \ket{r_i;k}$ back onto the sector basis:

\begin{prop}[rep-walk matrix element]\label{prop:repwalk}
With $r_b = \min_{a} a(s')$ the representative of the destination orbit and
$j$ its index,
\begin{equation}
  \bra{r_j;k} O_t \ket{r_i;k}
  \;=\;
  c_t \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{\nu_i}} \cdot
  \overline{\beta_{s'}} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{\nu_j}},
  \qquad
  \beta_{s'} \;=\; \sum_{\{h \in A \,:\, h(s') = r_b\}} \chi_k(h).
  \label{eq:repel}
\end{equation}
The sum over the $|A|$-fold orbit walk of the naive projector, and its
global $1/|A|$ weight, collapse into this single representative term.
\end{prop}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
Expand both projected states via \eqref{eq:projstate}; the double group sum
$\sum_{a,a'}$ reduces by substitution $h = a'^{-1} a$ to $|A|$ times a sum
over the coset $\{h : h(s') = r_b\}$; the $|A|$ cancels against the two
$1/\sqrt{|A|}$ normalizations.
\end{proof}

\textbf{Implementation.} The kernel-facing form is
\texttt{index\_and\_projection}: given a connected state, return the
destination index $j$ and the scalar
$\overline{\beta_{s'}}\, \nu_j^{-1/2}$ (the row factor $\nu_i^{-1/2}$ is
supplied as the kernel's \texttt{pre\_phase}). The host implementation
(\file{ed/matvec/rep\_symmetry\_basis\_policy.h}) computes all $|G|$ images
once into a stack buffer; one pass finds the minimum, one accumulates the
character sum:

\begin{lstlisting}
[[nodiscard]] inline std::int64_t
index_and_projection(std::uint64_t state, Complex& proj_out) const noexcept {
    if (n_up >= 0 && __builtin_popcountll(state) != n_up) return -1;
    if (group_size <= kMaxG) {           // kMaxG = 256
        std::uint64_t images[kMaxG];
        std::uint64_t rb = state;
        for (int g = 0; g < group_size; ++g) {
            images[g] = apply_perm(state, g);
            if (images[g] < rb) rb = images[g];
        }
        const std::int64_t k = index_of_rep(rb);
        if (k < 0) return -1;
        double acc_re = 0.0, acc_im = 0.0;
        for (int h = 0; h < group_size; ++h) {
            if (images[h] == rb) {
                acc_re += characters[h].real();   // conj: +real
                acc_im -= characters[h].imag();   //       -imag
            }
        }
        const double s = inv_norms[static_cast<std::size_t>(k)];
        proj_out = Complex(acc_re * s, acc_im * s);
        return k;
    }
    /* |G| > 256: two-pass, buffer-free fallback (same math) */
    ...
}
\end{lstlisting}

The reverse lookup \texttt{index\_of\_rep} is a binary search on the sorted
\texttt{reps} (zero memory), upgraded to $O(1)$ by two budget-gated
structures: a per-sector dense table indexed by the \emph{combinadic rank}
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{rank}(s) \;=\; \sum_{m=1}^{n_\uparrow} \binom{b_m}{m},
  \qquad b_1 < b_2 < \dots \ \text{the set-bit positions of } s,
  \label{eq:combinadic}
\end{equation}
or --- the production default at scale --- \emph{one} shared
$\mathrm{rank} \to \text{shared-rep-index}$ table per $(N, n_\uparrow)$
subspace co-owned by every irrep sector (\texttt{SharedRankLookup};
$77\,\mathrm{GB} \to 4.8\,\mathrm{GB}$ aggregate at $N=32$). The identical
arithmetic runs on the GPU (\texttt{DeviceRepSymmetryBasisPolicy},
\file{device\_basis\_policy.cuh}); full-space sectors ($n_\uparrow = -1$)
use the identity rank.

\subsection{One matvec representation, two sub-modes}
\label{sec:matvec}

Since Stage 11c-2b the representative kernel is the \emph{only} symmetry
matvec on both backends. Its two sub-modes trade memory for speed:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{RepReducedCsr (default).} Assemble the reduced sector matrix
  once by running \eqref{eq:repel} over every representative row
  (\file{ed/matvec/reduced\_symmetry\_csr.h}; OpenMP-parallel over rows),
  then every iteration is a plain $O(\mathrm{nnz})$ SpMV. The build costs
  one full group walk $\approx |G|$ SpMV-equivalents, so the breakeven
  against the walk is exactly $\text{iterations} = |G|$ --- always won by
  an iterative solver. Budget-gated per sector by an up-front upper bound
  against \texttt{ED\_SYM\_SECTOR\_CSR\_BUDGET\_GIB} (default 8), on
  \emph{both} lanes: an over-budget frontier sector falls back by itself.
\item \textbf{RepStream} (\texttt{ED\_SYM\_REDUCED\_CSR=0}, and the
  automatic over-budget fallback): regenerate \eqref{eq:repel} arithmetically
  on every apply. $O(\#\text{reps})$ memory --- the $N=36$ regime --- at
  $\sim|G|\times$ per-apply cost.
\end{itemize}

The budget gate is one consolidated helper, shared by both lanes
(\file{ed/planner/sym\_matvec\_policy\_hook.h}):

\begin{lstlisting}
[[nodiscard]] inline bool sector_csr_within_budget(
        std::uint64_t dim, std::uint64_t terms_per_row) noexcept {
    const std::uint64_t est_bytes =
        dim * terms_per_row * (16u /* complex value */ + 4u /* col idx */)
        + (dim + 1) * 8u /* row ptr */;
    double budget_gib = 8.0;
    if (const char* v = std::getenv("ED_SYM_SECTOR_CSR_BUDGET_GIB")) {
        const double b = std::atof(v);
        if (b > 0.0) budget_gib = b;
    }
    return static_cast<double>(est_bytes)
           <= budget_gib * static_cast<double>(1ULL << 30);
}
\end{lstlisting}

The builder \texttt{build\_reduced\_symmetry\_csr\_rep}
(\file{ed/matvec/reduced\_symmetry\_csr.h}) assembles row $r$ by applying
every Hamiltonian term to the single representative
$\texttt{rep}_r$ and resolving each connected state through
\texttt{index\_and\_projection} --- \emph{verbatim} the same element math
as the streaming kernel, which is what makes the two sub-modes bitwise
comparable (pinned by \file{test\_reduced\_symmetry\_csr.cpp} and the
integration strategy-parity test). Rows are independent, so the build is
OpenMP-parallel. Dense consumers (\texttt{solver="full"} on a sector)
densify the same reduced CSR; the eager orbit-walk dense assembler was
deleted with the orbit lane. On the GPU the resident mirror holds
$\texttt{reps} + \texttt{inv\_norms} + \text{perms} + \chi$
($\sim$600\,MB at $N=32$) and regenerates the group action inside the
kernel; measured end-to-end at $N=24$: rep mirror 4.8\,s vs the retired
orbit-CSR mirror's 18.2\,s.

% =============================================================================
\section{The flip extension \texorpdfstring{$A' = A \rtimes \Z_2$}{A' = A x Z2}}
% =============================================================================
\label{sec:flip}

The global spin flip $X = \prod_i \sigma^x_i$ commutes with every site
permutation, so when $[H, X] = 0$ it extends the \emph{abelian} factor ---
never the point-group residue: $A' = A \times \langle X \rangle$, realized
on bitstrings by the all-ones XOR mask.

\begin{lem}[closure rule]\label{lem:flip}
$X$ preserves the diagonal subspace iff the subspace is flip-invariant:
the $n_\uparrow$ block only at $n_\uparrow = N/2$; an $S^z$-parity half only
for even $N$ (X changes $n_\uparrow$ by $N - 2n_\uparrow$, of parity
$N \bmod 2$); the full space always. Elsewhere $X$ is a block mapper and
contributes only \emph{transport}: $\mathrm{spec}\, H|_{n_\uparrow} =
\mathrm{spec}\, H|_{N-n_\uparrow}$ (Prop.~\ref{prop:projfold}\,(ii)).
\end{lem}

This rule is stated \emph{once}
(\texttt{flip\_subspace\_admissible}, \file{ed/symmetry/spin\_flip.h}) and
consumed by the engine and every binding gate. Where admissible, the
extended characters are \emph{synthesized}, never re-decomposed: elements
are laid out $[A,\ A\!\cdot\!X]$ and
\begin{equation}
  \chi'_{k,s}(a X^f) \;=\; \chi_k(a)\, (\pm 1)^f, \qquad s \in \{+,-\},
  \label{eq:chiprime}
\end{equation}
doubling the irrep count ($k_{\mathrm{ext}} = k + s\, n_{\mathrm{irr}}$) and
halving the big sector:

\begin{lstlisting}
// Extended-irrep characters chi'_{k,s}(a + f|A|) = chi_k(a) * (s ? -1 : +1)^f.
[[nodiscard]] std::vector<Complex>
characters_for(const EngineContext& cx, int k_ext) {
    const auto& base =
        cx.giA.irreps[static_cast<std::size_t>(k_ext % cx.n_irr_raw)].character;
    std::vector<Complex> chi(base.begin(), base.end());
    if (cx.flip_half) {
        const double s = (k_ext / cx.n_irr_raw == 0) ? 1.0 : -1.0;
        chi.reserve(2 * base.size());
        for (const Complex& c : base) chi.push_back(s * c);
    }
    return chi;
}
\end{lstlisting}

Everything downstream --- norms \eqref{eq:projstate}, the walk
\eqref{eq:repel}, the reduced CSR, the GPU mirror --- is automatically
flip-correct because the \texttt{CompiledGroup} carries the XOR masks
inside \texttt{apply} and the characters carry the sign. Note that the
covering sum rule \eqref{eq:burnside} is \emph{unchanged} by the flip: the
flip halves sectors, not the subspace --- this makes it the Stage-9a
tripwire rather than a quantity to ``fix''.

% =============================================================================
\section{The little-group engine (lane A)}
% =============================================================================
\label{sec:littlegroup}

The full automorphism group is $G = A' \rtimes P$ with $P$ the retained
non-abelian residue (e.g.\ reflections/rotations). Lane A exploits $P$
\emph{maximally}: block-mapping residues fold sectors into stars; residues
that fix a sector are projected via its little co-group. All of this lives
in \file{src/solvers/cpu/little\_group\_solve.cpp} ($\sim$1.4\,kLOC).

\subsection{Stars: residue conjugation and the union--find fold}

A residue $p$ normalizes $A'$, so conjugation $c_p(a) = p^{-1} a p$
permutes the abelian irreps: $\chi_k \mapsto \chi_k \circ c_p$. Sectors
$k$ and $k^p$ are unitarily equivalent
(Prop.~\ref{prop:projfold}\,(ii) with $U = U_p$), with equal dimensions by
conjugation-invariance of $|\nu_r|$. One union--find over the extended
irreps, with edges from every residue map \emph{and} from time reversal
(\S\ref{sec:tr}), partitions the sector space into \textbf{stars}; each
star is solved once at its representative $k_0$ with multiplicity
$|\text{star}|$. Under the flip, residues act parity-diagonally
($\chi'_{k,s} \mapsto \chi'_{k^p,s}$): stars never mix flip parities.

\subsection{Monomial little co-group}

The little co-group of $k_0$ is
$L = \{\,p \in P : \chi_{k_0} \circ c_p = \chi_{k_0}\,\}$ modulo $A'$.
Its elements act on the representative basis by \emph{monomial matrices}:

\begin{prop}\label{prop:monomial}
For $p \in L$ and a surviving representative $r_i$, let
$s = \pi_p(r_i)$, let $r_b = \min_a a(s)$ with witness $a^*$
($a^*(s) = r_b$), and let $j$ index $r_b$. Then
\[
  U_p \ket{\psi_i(k_0)} \;=\;
  \overline{\chi_{k_0}(a^*)}\,
  \frac{\nu_j^{1/2}}{\nu_i^{1/2}}\,
  \ket{\psi_j(k_0)},
\]
and the coefficient has unit modulus. Coset representatives suffice:
$M_{a p} = \chi_{k_0}(a)\, M_p$.
\end{prop}

\begin{lstlisting}
// Monomial action of residue p on the k0 rep basis (build_monomial).
for (std::size_t i = 0; i < dim; ++i) {
    const std::uint64_t s = applyPermutation(rd.reps[i], p);
    std::uint64_t rb    = ~std::uint64_t{0};
    std::size_t   astar = 0;
    for (std::size_t a = 0; a < nA; ++a) {          // byte-LUT walk over A'
        const std::uint64_t img = cx.cg.apply(s, a);
        if (img < rb) { rb = img; astar = a; }
    }
    const auto it = std::lower_bound(rd.reps.begin(), rd.reps.end(), rb);
    if (it == rd.reps.end() || *it != rb) return false;      // fallback
    const std::size_t j = static_cast<std::size_t>(it - rd.reps.begin());
    // U_p |psi_i> = chi(b) (N_j/N_i) |psi_j>,  b = a*^{-1}
    const Complex ph = std::conj(chi[astar])
                     * (rd.inv_norms[i] / rd.inv_norms[j]);
    if (std::abs(std::abs(ph) - 1.0) > 1e-8) return false;   // must be unit
    out.to[i]    = static_cast<std::int32_t>(j);
    out.phase[i] = ph / std::abs(ph);
}
\end{lstlisting}

Each accepted monomial is then \emph{numerically validated} by a random-vector
commutator probe $\lVert [M_p, H_{k_0}] x \rVert / \lVert H M x \rVert <
10^{-8}$ (\texttt{monomial\_commutes}): a convention error or a residue that
does not truly fix the sector costs only the refinement, never correctness.

\subsection{Abstract decomposition and isotypic bases}

The monomials $\{M_l\}_{l \in L}$ realize $L$ only projectively in general;
the engine therefore decomposes the \emph{abstract} group from its
multiplication table (little co-groups need no faithful permutation
realization). \texttt{decompose\_irreps\_tables} computes the irreducible
characters $\chi_\sigma$ of $L$ from the regular representation's commutant;
the isotypic projectors
\begin{equation}
  P_\sigma \;=\; \frac{d_\sigma}{|L|} \sum_{l \in L}
  \overline{\chi_\sigma(l)}\, M_l
  \label{eq:isotypic}
\end{equation}
are applied per \emph{index orbit} of the monomial permutation structure,
and sparse orthonormal bases $W_\sigma$ of their ranges are extracted by
small SVDs. The block solved is $W_\sigma^\dagger H_{k_0} W_\sigma$ with
eigenvalue multiplicity $|\text{star}| \times d_\sigma$; the covering check
$\sum_\sigma (\#\mathrm{cols}\, W_\sigma) \, d_\sigma = \dim k_0$ guards
completeness. \textbf{Fallback ladder}: projective factor system,
non-closing residues, failed probe, incomplete covering $\Rightarrow$ solve
the plain $k_0$ block --- less reduction, never wrong physics.

\subsection{Time reversal: the fold that never projects}
\label{sec:tr}

For $H$ real in the computational basis, complex conjugation $K$ satisfies
$K H K = H$. In the representative basis, $K$ maps sector $k$ to $\bar k$
(characters conjugate), giving $H_{\bar k} = \overline{H_k}$ ---
isospectral, so $k \leftrightarrow \bar k$ edges join the star union--find.
Inside a self-conjugate star whose $\chi_{k_0}$ is \emph{all-real} (hence
$H_{k_0}$ real and the monomial phases real), conjugation maps the isotypic
component $\sigma$ to $\sigma^*$: $\overline{W_\sigma}$ spans the
$\sigma^*$ isotypic, so conjugate pairs solve \emph{once} at doubled
multiplicity. Both folds are exact copies, guarded (equal $W$ column
counts, covering accounting) and revert to solving both on any doubt.

\subsection{Routing (lane A vs lane B)}

One decision point, \texttt{resolve\_projection\_lane}
(\file{point\_group\_routing.py}): \texttt{point\_group="auto"} (default)
projects every eigenvalue-only call; declines --- eigenvectors,
\texttt{sector=}, sampling methods, an explicit GPU request, no residue,
\texttt{ED\_SYM\_LITTLE\_GROUP=0} --- degrade silently to lane B
(the abelian composition lane: same role assignment, fold-heavy, serving
vectors/sampling/GPU/per-sector metadata). \texttt{"full"} raises with the
decline reason; no fallback engine exists (the monolithic
symmetry-adapted-basis engine survives only as a parity-test oracle).
Explicit non-abelian generator lists are closed (cap 4096) and carved into
(maximal-abelian core, coset residues) by \texttt{split\_nonabelian}.

\section{Lane B: the abelian composition lane}
% =============================================================================
\label{sec:laneb}

Lane B serves every consumer the projection engine declines: eigenvector
requests, per-sector metadata, sampling methods (FTLM/TPQ), and the GPU. It
assigns the \emph{same} roles as lane A but exploits the block-mappers as
folds over the abelian sector set rather than projecting little co-groups.

\textbf{Composition plan} (\file{ed/symmetry/sector\_plan.h}). Pure planning
functions shared by the thermal flat pool and the ground-state lane:
\texttt{resolve\_symmetry\_composition} normalizes the per-call toggles
(\texttt{spin\_flip=}, \texttt{time\_reversal=} in
$\{\texttt{auto},\texttt{on},\texttt{off},\texttt{require}\}$, one SymToggle
convention shared with the engine) against term-level detection
($[H, X] = 0$ checker; $H$-real checker); \texttt{plan\_build\_window}
shrinks the $n_\uparrow$ sweep to $n_\uparrow \le N/2$ under flip transport
(Lemma~\ref{lem:flip}(ii), spectra mirrored to $N - n_\uparrow$ with tags
restored); \texttt{plan\_tr\_actions} plus the point-group \emph{star maps}
(how each residue conjugates the abelian irreps, pure permutation algebra
computed in Python) feed \emph{one} union--find
(\texttt{sector\_orbit\_canonical}) that folds TR pairing
$k \leftrightarrow \bar k$ and all residue maps $k \mapsto k^p$ into
isospectral orbits --- solve one representative per orbit, copy the
spectrum. The fold arithmetic is flip-slot aware: synthetic indices
$k + s\, n_{\mathrm{irr}}$ pair within parity blocks.

\textbf{In-sector flip projection.} At $n_\uparrow = N/2$ (or on parity
halves / the full space) lane B splits each momentum sector into
$(k, \pm)$ slots using the same extended characters \eqref{eq:chiprime};
the flip-projected sectors have \emph{no orbit-CSR form at all} (the
provider throws by design) --- they exist only in the representative
representation, which is why the one-matvec consolidation
(\S\ref{sec:matvec}) was a precondition for this machinery rather than an
optimization.

\textbf{Labels.} Both lanes emit an honest per-eigenvalue attribution: the
engine pools $(k_{\mathrm{raw}}, \text{flip parity}, \sigma, d_\sigma)$
labels plus the raw-irrep character table (the engine's internal irrep
order is \emph{not} the directory sector order; momentum is decoded from
the phase of $\chi_k(T)$, convention $e^{-2\pi i k/N}$), and lane B carries
per-sector eigenvalue arrays and \texttt{SectorTag}s. The pooled-vs-tagged
fork is a documented output contract, not an accident.

\textbf{Ground-state arbitration.} The cross-irrep bindings locate the
ground sector by a two-phase scan (\texttt{find\_gs\_sector\_two\_phase}):
a cheap 40-iteration Lanczos per sector prunes to candidates within a gap
window of the running minimum; survivors re-solve at $10^{-12}$; the
winner's eigenpair must then pass the residual gate of
\S\ref{sec:dssf} before any weight is computed. Phase-1 failures mark a
sector $-\infty$ (never silently dropped), and a non-finite phase-1 sweep
falls back to scanning everything.

\section{Krylov numerics: Lanczos, ghosts, and the July 2026 fix}
% =============================================================================
\label{sec:lanczos}

\subsection{The kernel}

One templated implementation, \texttt{ed::krylov::lanczos\_kernel<Backend>}
(\file{ed/krylov/lanczos\_kernel.h}), is \emph{the} Lanczos step for every
consumer (ground state, FTLM/LTLM inner loops, continued fractions, thermal
pure states). The three-term recurrence
\begin{equation}
  \beta_{j+1} v_{j+1} \;=\; H v_j - \alpha_j v_j - \beta_j v_{j-1},
  \qquad \alpha_j = v_j^\dagger H v_j,
  \label{eq:lanczos}
\end{equation}
builds $T_m = \mathrm{tridiag}(\beta; \alpha; \beta)$ whose eigenvalues
(Ritz values) approximate $\mathrm{spec}(H)$ from the extremes inward.
Reorthogonalization policies: \texttt{None}, \texttt{LocalDGKS$k$} (a ring
of the last $k$ basis vectors, re-orthogonalized twice, DGKS-style),
\texttt{PeriodicCGS2}, \texttt{FullCGS2}; options carry
\texttt{convergence\_check} callbacks, \texttt{aux\_ortho\_ptrs} (project
out a fixed external set --- used by thick-restart Krylov--Schur locking),
\texttt{dim\_cap}, and \texttt{keep\_basis=false} for value-only runs.

\subsection{The ghost mechanism}

In exact arithmetic the $v_j$ are orthonormal and every Ritz value of $T_m$
is a Rayleigh--Ritz approximation from a genuine subspace. In finite
precision, orthogonality is lost \emph{precisely when a Ritz pair
converges} (Paige): the recurrence re-introduces components along converged
directions, and $T_m$ then carries \emph{ghost copies} --- spurious
duplicates of converged eigenvalues, and transiently, spurious values
between genuine levels. Two structural consequences matter for a
\emph{block} solver that requests the lowest $k$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item ghosts can sit \emph{exactly on} true levels, so a value-membership
  test passes while the returned \emph{multiset} is wrong (duplicated
  levels displace true ones from the $k$-window);
\item near subspace exhaustion ($m \to \dim$, unavoidable on small blocks)
  ghost formation is generic even with ring reorthogonalization.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The bug and the fix (commit \texttt{7651683})}

The little-group per-block lowest-$k$ solver, \texttt{solve\_block\_lowest},
had delegated to a legacy wrapper with an iteration budget
$\texttt{max\_it} = 2k + 40$ and \emph{no convergence guard} --- on
near-degenerate blocks every block ran to its cap and returned whatever
Ritz values $T_{60}$ held. Verified both ways on the $4\times4$
$J_1$--$J_2$ model ($J_2 = 0.15$, $n_\uparrow = 8$, $k = 10$): the pre-fix
body returns the multiset $\{E_0^{\times 2},\ E_1^{\times 2},\ E_2,\
(-8.2328)^{\times 5}\}$ against the true
$\{E_0, E_1, E_2, (-8.2328)^{\times 4}, (-8.0992)^{\times 3}\}$ ---
multiset error $1.4$; the fixed body reproduces the dense window to
$1.3\times 10^{-13}$.

The replacement: below the crossover $\max(\texttt{dense\_max\_dim},\,64k)$
a dense values-only eigensolve (near-exhaustion territory: exact and
effectively free); above it, the kernel with \texttt{LocalDGKS3} (ring 8),
no stored basis, a real budget $\max(40k, 400)$, and an
\emph{all-$k$-lowest Ritz stationarity gate} --- the shared smallest-only
predicate would strand the upper levels of the window:

\begin{lstlisting}
kopts.convergence_check =
    [prev, kk](const std::vector<double>& alpha,
               const std::vector<double>& beta) -> bool {
        const int m = static_cast<int>(alpha.size());
        if (static_cast<std::size_t>(m) < kk + 2) return false;
        /* assemble T_m, dense-solve values-only (Eigen) ... */
        std::vector<double> cur(es.eigenvalues().data(),
                                es.eigenvalues().data() + nk);
        bool conv = prev->size() == cur.size();
        for (std::size_t i = 0; conv && i < cur.size(); ++i) {
            const double den = std::max(std::abs(cur[i]), 1e-300);

\end{lstlisting}

The regression pin (\file{test\_little\_group\_lowest\_window.py}) compares
the pooled lowest-$k$ against the pinned dense reference \emph{as sorted
multisets} --- element-wise on sorted arrays, so one ghost duplicate shifts
every subsequent entry.

\section{Finite temperature and spectra}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{Thermal kernels}

All backend-templated headers under \file{ed/thermal/}, driven by
\texttt{ed::workflows::thermal}:

\textbf{FTLM} (finite-temperature Lanczos): for $R$ random states $\ket{r}$
with per-state Krylov eigenpairs $(\varepsilon_j^{(r)}, \ket{\psi_j^{(r)}})$,
\begin{equation}
  Z \approx \frac{D}{R} \sum_{r=1}^{R} \sum_j
    e^{-\beta \varepsilon_j^{(r)}} \bigl|\braket{r}{\psi_j^{(r)}}\bigr|^2,
  \qquad
  \langle H \rangle \approx \frac{D}{RZ} \sum_{r,j}
    \varepsilon_j^{(r)} e^{-\beta \varepsilon_j^{(r)}}
    \bigl|\braket{r}{\psi_j^{(r)}}\bigr|^2 .
\end{equation}
\textbf{LTLM} refines the low-$T$ end from a single ground-state-seeded
chain. \textbf{mTPQ} (microcanonical thermal pure quantum): iterate
$\ket{\phi_{m+1}} \propto (L - \hat h)\ket{\phi_m}$ with
$\hat h = H/N$; the inverse temperature of the $m$-th iterate follows from
its energy moments,
$\beta_m \simeq 2m / \bigl(N (L - \langle \hat h \rangle_m)\bigr)$, and
$C_v = \beta^2 \mathrm{Var}(H)$. The fp32 GPU lane
(\file{mtpq\_f32.h}) halves the vector footprint for the frontier.

\textbf{Trajectory aggregation} (\file{ed/thermal/tpq\_thermo.h}): samples'
$(\beta_k, E_k, \mathrm{var}_k)$ trajectories are interpolated at
$\beta = 1/T$ and Welford-averaged; \emph{absolute} entropy and free energy
come from thermodynamic integration anchored at the exact infinite-$T$
point:
\begin{equation}
  \ln Z(\beta) \;=\; \ln D \;-\; \int_0^\beta \langle E \rangle(\beta')\,
  d\beta', \qquad
  S = \beta \langle E \rangle + \ln Z, \qquad F = -\ln Z / \beta,
  \label{eq:lnz}
\end{equation}
with the $[0, \beta_1]$ head segment anchored at
$\langle E\rangle(0) = \mathrm{Tr}\,H / D$ (recovered for free from every
sample's $\beta \approx 0$ first trajectory point). The absolute $F_s$ is
what makes per-sector free energies usable as Boltzmann weights in the
$U(1)$/spatial recombination.

\textbf{KPM-DOS}: Chebyshev moments of the rescaled Hamiltonian with
Jackson damping and a Hutchinson stochastic trace; the moment and
random-vector counts are forwarded knobs (a silently-defaulted $M{=}2048,
R{=}20$ was one of the converter-fork casualties fixed in Stage 11a).

\subsection{Ground-state dynamical structure factors}
\label{sec:dssf}

The zero-temperature spectral function of a probe $O$ is
\begin{equation}
  S_O(\omega) = \sum_n |\bra{n} O \ket{0}|^2\, \delta(\omega - (E_n - E_0))
  = -\tfrac{1}{\pi} \operatorname{Im}
    \bra{\phi} \bigl(\omega + i\eta + E_0 - H\bigr)^{-1} \ket{\phi},
  \quad \ket{\phi} = O\ket{0},
\end{equation}
evaluated as a continued fraction from the Lanczos coefficients of $H$
seeded with $\ket{\phi}$:
\begin{equation}
  \bra{\phi}(z - H)^{-1}\ket{\phi} \;=\;
  \cfrac{\lVert\phi\rVert^2}{z - \alpha_0 -
    \cfrac{\beta_1^2}{z - \alpha_1 - \cfrac{\beta_2^2}{\;\ddots}}}\ .
  \label{eq:cf}
\end{equation}
In the symmetry-resolved pipeline, $\ket{0}$ is located by the star walk
and solved \emph{plain} in its momentum sector (residual-guarded); then
$O\ket{0}$ is \emph{scattered} into every raw destination sector of every
reachable diagonal subspace ($n_\uparrow \pm \Delta$, parity halves, full
space) through the rectangular representative apply
(\texttt{CrossSectorOrbitObservable}): source orbits regenerate per group
element, the destination projects through
\texttt{index\_and\_projection}. Selection rules are \emph{physical}:
$\lVert \phi_{\mathrm{dst}} \rVert < \varepsilon$ means the channel is
forbidden; no selection-rule plumbing exists, and the weights obey
$\sum_{\mathrm{dst}} \lVert\phi_{\mathrm{dst}}\rVert^2 =
\lVert O\ket{0}\rVert^2$. For real $H$, a $-Q$ panel whose probe is the
coefficient-conjugate of a computed $+Q$ probe is copied exactly:
$\bra{n}\bar O\ket{0} = \overline{\bra{n} O \ket{0}}$ in a real eigenbasis.

\textbf{Residual guards.} Both GS re-solve sites in the cross-irrep
bindings enforce
$\lVert H\psi_0 - E_0 \psi_0\rVert / \lVert\psi_0\rVert < 10^{-8}$ before
any weight is computed --- the orchestrator's warn-not-fail contract
otherwise returns a best-effort pair whose garbage the scattered weights
and the continued fraction inherit silently:

\begin{lstlisting}
template <class SectorView>
inline void require_gs_residual(SectorView& sec,
                                const std::vector<Complex>& psi0, double E0) {
    const std::size_t d = psi0.size();
    std::vector<Complex> hv(d, Complex(0.0, 0.0));
    sec.apply(psi0.data(), hv.data(), d);
    double num = 0.0, den = 0.0;
    for (std::size_t i = 0; i < d; ++i) {
        const Complex r = hv[i] - E0 * psi0[i];
        num += std::norm(r);  den += std::norm(psi0[i]);
    }
    const double resid = (den > 0.0) ? std::sqrt(num / den)
                                     : std::numeric_limits<double>::infinity();
    if (!(resid < 1e-8)) throw std::runtime_error(/* loud, actionable */);
}
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Correctness never rides on bookkeeping}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Covering sum rule} \eqref{eq:burnside}: after every fold and
  projection, pooled multiplicities must tile the subspace dimension.
\item \textbf{Monomial probe}: every accepted $M_p$ passes the numerical
  $[M_p, H_{k_0}] = 0$ random-vector test; the isotypic covering
  $\sum_\sigma |W_\sigma| d_\sigma = \dim k_0$ is checked before any block
  replaces the plain solve.
\item \textbf{Residual gates}: the engine's ground-state factory throws on
  a stale Ritz pair; both cross-irrep GS sites run
  \texttt{require\_gs\_residual}; the lowest-$k$ window is
  stationarity-gated (\S\ref{sec:lanczos}).
\item \textbf{Oracles}: routed verbs $\equiv$ the retired monolithic SAB
  engine $\equiv$ dense references, pinned across
  \file{test\_little\_group*.py}, \file{test\_point\_group\_routing.py},
  and multiset window pins. One local gate
  (\file{scripts/check\_local.sh}) runs every CI lane.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hash quality is a correctness concern}
\label{sec:hashing}

Content-keyed registries (orbit tables, GPU mirrors, SAB cache) briefly
keyed on a word-XOR FNV digest. That mix is \emph{degenerate under
character sign patterns}: on a $\Z_8$ ring,
$\chi_{k+4}(g) = (-1)^g \chi_k(g)$, and XOR-folding the words of two such
character vectors collides --- observed as GPU sectors $5/7$ silently
reusing the mirrors of $1/3$. Two independent fixes, both required:
(i) every key material word passes through the \texttt{splitmix64}
avalanche,
\begin{lstlisting}
z = (z ^ (z >> 30)) * 0xbf58476d1ce4e5b9ULL;
z = (z ^ (z >> 27)) * 0x94d049bb133111ebULL;
return z ^ (z >> 31);
\end{lstlisting}
and (ii) \emph{no hit is trusted}: mirror slots verify a full identity
fingerprint; orbit-table registry and disk hits are physically re-verified
(\texttt{orbit\_table\_consistent}: sampled representatives checked for
subspace membership and canonical minimality under the caller's group),
with loud eviction and rebuild on failure.
