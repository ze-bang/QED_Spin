# QED_Spin

Exact diagonalization of spin-1/2 Hamiltonians on the CPU and on one NVIDIA GPU: lowest
levels and eigenvectors, full spectra, thermodynamics (exact, FTLM, mTPQ, ⟨O⟩(T)) and
dynamical correlations at zero and finite temperature, each resolved by every symmetry
the Hamiltonian has.
